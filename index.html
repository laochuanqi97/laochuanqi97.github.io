About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The University of Texas at Arlington, advised by Prof. Junzhou Huang.

My research interests focus on large language models and their applications in biology and medicine. I am interested in using machine learning to address biomedical problems.

My published work includes cancer drug response prediction and drug–target interaction prediction.

Before joining UTA, I was responsible for webpage data mining and foundational data infrastructure development within Alibaba's Qwen Business Unit. My work focused on building standardized, modular webpage databases and associated feature repositories, using business-driven multimodal LLMs to transform heterogeneous webpage content into structured, reusable features and data assets supporting Qwen large language models and a range of business applications.
